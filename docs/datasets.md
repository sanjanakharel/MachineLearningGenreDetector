# Datasets

## Chosen dataset
- **FMA (Free Music Archive), Medium subset.** 25,000 clips of 30 seconds each, 16 genres (unbalanced).
  - Repo and docs: https://github.com/mdeff/fma
  - Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.01840
  - Includes precomputed Librosa features (`features.csv`) and official train/validation/test splits.

## Catalogs and alternatives we looked at
- MIR datasets catalog: https://github.com/ismir/mir-datasets/blob/master/outputs/mir-datasets.md
- Kaggle music genre classification: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/purumalgi/music-genre-classification
- IRMAS (instrument recognition): https://www.upf.edu/web/mtg/irmas
